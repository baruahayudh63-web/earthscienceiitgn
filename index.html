Pioneering Earth System Science

Advancing Knowledge & Impact

The Department of Earth Sciences at IIT Gandhinagar builds a quantitative, physics-based understanding of Earth systems. We bridge the gap between ancient geological processes and pressing modern challenges in climate, water, and energy.

  • M.Sc., M.Tech. & Ph.D. Degrees: World-class education with fieldwork and lab exposure.
  • Nature Index 2026 5th Rank: Recognized among India's top academic earth science research departments.
  • ANRF Centre of Excellence: Leading interdisciplinary research in archaeology and traditional knowledge systems.
