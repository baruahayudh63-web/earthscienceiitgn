🏛️ Institutional Overview

About the Department of Earth Sciences

Established to address global earth science challenges, our department bridges fundamental earth processes with societal impact in water resources, climate resilience, and natural hazard assessment.

🎯

Our Vision

To be a globally recognized center of excellence in Earth Sciences education, interdisciplinary scientific research, and technological innovation. We strive to nurture future leaders capable of solving complex planetary challenges through empirical inquiry, modeling, and fieldwork.

🚀

Our Mission

To conduct fundamental and applied research on Earth systems, foster interdisciplinary academic programs, train high-caliber earth scientists, and partner with industrial, governmental, and international academic organizations to serve humanity.

Core Focus Areas

Strategic Research Pillars

Our research portfolio spans interconnected Earth domains from deep crustal geodynamics to atmospheric water cycles.

🌊

Water Security & Climate Hydrogeology

Quantifying groundwater depletion, river discharge extremes, monsoon modeling, and ANRF Centre of Excellence water initiatives across South Asia.

⛰️

Tectonics & Surface Processes

Investigating Himalayan uplift, river channel dynamics, fault movement, sediment transport, and active tectonics in western India.

🏺

Geoarchaeology & Palaeoclimate

Reconstructing past climate variability, ancient Harappan settlements, river dynamics, and human-environmental interactions over millennia.

🪨

Carbonate Geology & Coastal Systems

High-resolution micro-petrography, sea-level reconstruction, micro-CT scanning of reef systems, and reservoir characterization.

📊

Remote Sensing & GIS Analytics

Utilizing satellite imagery, LiDAR, SAR interferometry, and spatial machine learning for hazard mapping and land cover monitoring.

🌋

Igneous Petrology & Geochemistry

Analyzing mantle geochemistry, Deccan Traps volcanism, mineral crystallization, and geochemical trace element signatures.

Recognitions & Impact

Departmental Achievements

Faculty Excellence

Industrial Synergy
