Department Overview

About the Department

The Department of Earth Sciences at IIT Gandhinagar advances quantitative research and education across Earth system processes, linking contemporary climate challenges with deep geological time.

Our Mission & Vision

We aim to develop a fundamental and quantitative understanding of lithosphere, hydrosphere, atmosphere, and biosphere processes through basic and applied physical sciences.

Our research directly informs critical scientific, industrial, and societal challenges — from river geomorphology and water resilience to earthquake geodynamics, carbon capture, and geoarchaeology.

  • Quantitative Science: Integrating field mapping, high-pressure experimentation, and physics-guided machine learning.
  • Societal Solutions: Addressing water security, flood management, climate resilience, and sustainable energy transition.
  • Inclusive Education: Nurturing curiosity, discovery, and interdisciplinary problem solving in students.
